## About this site

In September 2015, the UN General Assembly adopted the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable
Development, an action plan for people, the planet, and prosperity, which also aims to
strengthen universal peace and access to justice. This agenda is detailed in 17 Sustainable
Development Goals (SDGs) and 169 targets for the year 2030.

Pablo de Olavide University (UPO), like other organizations, has found in the SDGs a
roadmap to align the university&#39;s work with the major challenges of the planet and its
people, &#39;leaving no one behind&#39;.

The UPO&#39;s sustainable development indicators panel includes the technical aspects of
monitoring UPO2030 and the working methodology for its measurement, as well as the
objectives set out in the Action Plan for the year 2030. It also integrates information
related to the sectoral plans that make it up (Equality, Inclusion, and Planet).

The Rector&#39;s Delegate for the Sustainable Campus is responsible for coordinating the
implementation of the 2030 Agenda at our university and monitoring it. Therefore, it is
responsible for this Control Panel and its maintenance.

The main objectives of the Sustainable Campus Delegation, all linked to the A2030, are:

- Leadership and coordination of environmental policy and the green infrastructure
  of the campus.
- Leadership and coordination of projects and initiatives aimed at improving energy
  efficiency and reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
- Management of the Campus of International Excellence (CEI-CAMBIO).
- Leadership and coordination of initiatives in the field of environmental education.
- Leadership and coordination of initiatives aimed at maintaining the biodiversity of
  the campus.
- Leadership and coordination of sustainable mobility policy on campus.
- Promotion of agreements, conventions, and specific contracts in environmental
  matters with other institutions, bodies, or public or private companies.
