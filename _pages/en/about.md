## About this site

In September 2015, the UN General Assembly adopted the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development, a plan of action for people, the planet, and prosperity, which also aims to strengthen universal peace and access to justice. This agenda is embodied in 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and 169 targets for the year 2030.

The Pablo de Olavide University, like other organizations, has found in the SDGs a roadmap to align the university's work with the major challenges of the planet and its people, 'leaving no one behind.'

The UPO's sustainable development indicator panel outlines the technical aspects of monitoring UPO2030 and the methodology for its measurement, as well as the objectives set in the Action Plan for 2030. It also integrates information related to the sectoral plans that make it up (Equality, Inclusion, and Planet).

The **Rector's Delegate for Sustainable Campus** is responsible for coordinating the implementation of the 2030 Agenda at our university and monitoring it. Thus, it is in charge of this Control Panel and its maintenance.

The main objectives of the Sustainable Campus Delegation, all linked to the A2030, are:

- Directing and coordinating the campus's environmental policy and green infrastructure.
- Directing and coordinating projects and initiatives aimed at improving energy efficiency and reducing greenhouse gas emissions.
- Managing the International Excellence Campus (CEI-CAMBIO).
- Directing and coordinating initiatives in the field of environmental education.
- Directing and coordinating initiatives aimed at maintaining the campus's biodiversity.
- Directing and coordinating the sustainable mobility policy on campus.
- Promoting agreements, conventions, and specific contracts on environmental matters with other institutions, organizations, or public or private companies.
